SnackStack

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

SnackStack is a personal grocery tracker for shopping lists, pantry items and grocery spending. This page explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and what control you have over it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them only to sign you in.
  • What you enter: shopping list items, pantry items, purchases and prices, food waste entries, and your settings (currency and monthly budget).

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data.

Where your data goes

Data Where it's stored or sent
Your account and everything you enter A database hosted by Supabase. Access rules on every table mean only your signed-in account can read or change your data.
Receipt photos Nowhere. Receipts are read on your device, and the photo is never uploaded or saved.
Pantry item names, when you use “What can I cook?” Sent to TheMealDB to search for recipes. Nothing that identifies you is sent.
Voice input Processed by your browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome, this uses Google's servers.
Your cart and theme preference Stored only in your browser on your device.
The app itself Served by Cloudflare, which may keep standard request logs.

Google user data

SnackStack requests only the basic Google profile scopes (name, email and profile picture) to create and sign in to your account. This data isn't used for any other purpose, isn't shared with anyone, and isn't used to train AI or machine learning models. SnackStack's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

  • You can edit or delete any item, purchase or entry in the app at any time.
  • You can export your purchases, pantry and food waste log as CSV files from Settings.
  • To delete your account and all of its data, open a request on the project's GitHub issues page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
